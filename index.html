Before your appointment

Preparing for your wax

A few simple steps make your Brazilian wax faster, smoother and more comfortable.

01

Skip the razor

Avoid shaving or tweezing for about two weeks so hair is long enough for the wax to grip.

02

Exfoliate gently

A light exfoliation one to two days before helps release trapped hairs and reduces ingrowns.

03

Plan for comfort

If you’re sensitive, ask your doctor or pharmacist whether an over-the-counter pain reliever before your visit is right for you.

04

Mind your cycle

Skin tends to be more sensitive just before and during your period — book around it when you can.

05

Wear something loose

Soft, breathable clothing keeps freshly waxed skin calm on your way home.

06

Talk to Erica

Share any medications, skin treatments or concerns — openness means a safer, better wax.
